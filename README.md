# louis

Independent ecological project on the spatiotemporal ecology of King Rail and other secretive marsh birds in southwest Louisiana.

## Scientific origin

This project grew from an EOG anomaly: future King Rail detection retained a small history-dependent signal, yet all six tested simple local-propagation worlds were falsified.

The important interpretation is **not** "King Rails moved farther than 17 km." EOG used observed detections as sources. With imperfect acoustic detection, a bird can remain at a site, go undetected, and later reappear as an apparent unsupported event.

## Current question

> **When secretive marsh-bird detections are temporally structured but simple local propagation fails, is that structure generated by persistent latent occupancy, habitat filtering, synchronized detectability, or genuinely open spatial dynamics?**

Immediate next steps:

1. classify every positive King Rail event as continuation, return after a detection gap, first detection near prior positives, or first detection outside prior observed-source support;
2. separate latent occupancy from acoustic detection;
3. compare same-site persistence, local-neighbour propagation, regional occasion effects, and open-population terms;
4. freeze the resulting temporal hypotheses before inspecting detailed response sequences of the remaining marsh-bird species.

## Evidence boundary

King Rail generated these hypotheses after its response was opened. Same-King-Rail mechanism analysis is therefore post-hoc diagnosis.

The remaining species can support a more prospective temporal-structure extension if their detailed sequences are kept unopened until the endpoint definitions are frozen; published habitat associations are already known and must not be described as blind.

EOG is provenance only. Ecological claims here require direct ecological evidence.
