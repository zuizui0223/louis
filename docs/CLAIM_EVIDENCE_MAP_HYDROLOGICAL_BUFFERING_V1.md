## Primary headline claim

> **Resident King Rails strongly buffer the water-depth variation they experience relative to contemporaneous local habitat availability.**

## Strongest quantitative support

> Across 190 matched events from 10 birds, the pooled within-bird used-on-availability slope was 0.162 versus a matched pseudo-used expectation of 1.001.

## Novelty boundary

Movement-environment coupling and habitat tracking are established topics.

The paper's contribution is the individual-level demonstration that **experienced environmental variation is damped relative to time-matched local availability inside resident home ranges**, not the invention of habitat tracking.

## Replication boundary

No external system currently reproduces the same event-matched SRI/coupling estimand.

Until that occurs, use:

- “independent Lake Erie result”
- “cross-individual consistency”
- “external biological consistency/context”

Do not use:

- “cross-system replicated SRI”
- “general law across wetland birds”
